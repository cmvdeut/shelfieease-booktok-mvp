## Business

ShelfieEase (shelfieease.app) is a browser-based ISBN book scanner and physical shelf tracker built for BookTok creators. Users scan barcodes on their phone, organize books on custom shelves (emoji + name), track TBR / Reading / Finished, and export a vertical 9:16 shelfie card for TikTok or Reels. No app download, no account required to start. Free up to 10 books; €4.99 one-time for unlimited. Data stays local in the browser with optional JSON backup.

Tagline: Scan · Shelf · Share

## Customer

The BookTok reader who buys physical books and has lost track of what they own. Often has a chaotic TBR pile, has bought the same book twice, or stands in a bookstore unsure if a title is already on their shelf. Posts shelf tours, hauls, current reads, or TBR content on TikTok or Instagram. Not looking for a social reading network — they want clarity on their physical collection and content-ready visuals without Canva hacks.

## Primary CTA

**Main:** Open shelfieease.app and scan your first book (free, no sign-up).

**Secondary (when the post is about community, not the product):** Follow @shelfieease on TikTok or Instagram.

**Only mention pricing when the hook is about duplicates or value:** €4.99 lifetime unlock — never lead with this.

## Strong Opinion / Wedge

**Lead with this:** You don't need another reading app with accounts and feeds. You need a tool for your physical shelf — scan in the store, organize your TBR, share a shelfie in 30 seconds.

Supporting angles:
- No account, no download, no friction — open browser and scan immediately
- Physical shelf-first, not social network-first
- €4.99 lifetime costs less than one accidental duplicate purchase
- Local-first privacy: your shelf, your device, your backup

## Voice

Warm and personal, playful and irreverent, scrappy underdog. BoekTok-native — POV hooks, relatable pain, emojis used naturally (📚 ✨ 💀 💅), short punchy lines. Dutch by default. Casual but clear, never corporate. Sounds like a fellow book lover talking to book lovers, not a SaaS landing page.

Five words: BookTok-native, zero friction, shelf-first.

## Universal Voice Rules (apply to every post)

- Contractions always ("don't" not "do not")
- Active voice, short sentences
- Address the reader as "you"
- Numbers as digits ("3 tips" not "three tips")
- No em dashes — use commas, periods, or "..."
- One concrete idea per post, not three
- Specific details over generic statements
